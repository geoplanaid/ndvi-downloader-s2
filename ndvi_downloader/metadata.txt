about=Draw an area or use a polygon layer (every feature is one area, named from a field of your choice) and the plugin writes one NDVI GeoTIFF per area per month.
    Each month is a per-pixel composite (median, maximum/MVC or mean) of every Sentinel-2 L2A scene of that month, with clouds, cloud shadows and cirrus masked using the Scene Classification Layer.
    Scenes come from the public Earth Search STAC catalogue (Element 84 / AWS Open Data): no account, API key or Google Earth Engine login needed, and only the pixels inside each area are read.
    Areas larger than the configured maximum are skipped, months with too little cloud-free coverage are skipped, existing files can be skipped to resume a run, and a CSV report lists every area and month.
    Output: float32 GeoTIFF in the local UTM zone at 10/20/30/60 m, clipped to the polygon, nodata -9999. Interface in Bahasa Indonesia.
